How we work

Blood drives, people and data, working together.

UHAI runs blood drives with the Eldoret blood bank, both at the bank itself and at outdoor sites. We turn drives into community events, and invite businesses in healthcare and allied industries, such as insurance, banking and sports, to take part.

Behind the drives sits a community of about {{ c.COMMUNITY_SIZE }} health professionals and students. They mobilise donors, run the drives, and build the predictive and behavioural-reward models that help us plan.

Our mobile application is designed to identify and retain donors and to link them with patients in need.
