Why UHAI

Every day in Kenya, patients wait for blood that hasn't been collected, can't be found, or has expired.

Supply is only part of the problem; the rest lies in the chain. UHAI addresses each link: getting donors in the door, keeping blood safe once it is collected, and making sure it reaches the patient who needs it.
