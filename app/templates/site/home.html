UHAI Life Sciences · {{ c.LOCATION }}

Safe blood, on time, for every patient who needs it.

We are a social enterprise strengthening Kenya's blood supply chain. We mobilise donors through blood drives, and use predictive modelling and better data so that blood is collected, stored and shared where it is needed most.

Learn About UHAI Go to Dashboard

The challenge

Kenya collects less than a third of the blood it needs.

Patients with cancer, blood disorders, trauma injuries or upcoming surgery depend on transfusions. When blood is short, their treatment is delayed, hospital stays get longer and lives are put at risk.

What UHAI does

We work on the whole journey, from donor to patient.

With the Eldoret blood bank, UHAI focuses on three links in the chain. Data and predictive modelling connect them, so that each drive, each fridge and each request is planned rather than improvised.
